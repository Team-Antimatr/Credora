SCORING METHODOLOGY

How Credora Evaluates Your Loan Profile

01

Applicant & Co-Applicant Income

Evaluates monthly earnings relative to the requested loan amount and repayment tenure to ensure comfortable EMI affordability.

02

Employment & Stability

Considers employment status (salaried vs. self-employed) and work continuity without penalizing non-traditional rural livelihoods.

03

Credit History & Existing Obligations

Analyzes past repayment behavior and active debt obligations to prevent both unfair rejections and high-risk approvals.

04

Property Area & Loan Purpose

Accounts for regional context across Urban, Semi-Urban, and Rural India so location never creates manual officer bias.

VERIFICATION CHECKLIST

Documents Needed for Final Officer Verification

Complete your instant digital pre-approval online first; keep these standard documents ready for final verification:

STANDARD KYC & INCOME

Identity & Income Proof (All Loans)

  • PAN Card & Aadhaar Card (KYC Verification)
  • Last 3 Months Salary Slips or Income Tax Returns (ITR)
  • Last 6 Months Bank Account Statement
  • Employment ID or Business Registration Proof
COLLATERAL & TITLE

Additional Documents (Home Loans Only)

  • Property Sale Agreement or Allotment Letter
  • Approved Building Plan & Title Deed Copy
  • Property Valuation & Land Revenue Records (Urban/Rural)
